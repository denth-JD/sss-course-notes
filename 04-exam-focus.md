## 1. How much does 1 m³ of soil weigh?

**Exam answer: about 1.5 to 2 tonnes per m³.** If the question gives options, choose the one in that range. If it asks for a single figure, write **about 2 tonnes** (roughly the weight of a car).

| Soil condition | Approx. weight per m³ |
| --- | --- |
| Loose, dry topsoil or sand | 1.2 – 1.6 t |
| Compacted fill, moist sand, gravel | 1.8 – 2.0 t |
| Wet or saturated clay | 2.0 – 2.2 t |

Engineering check: soil bulk unit weight is usually 16–20 kN/m³. Divide by 9.81 to get 1.6–2.0 t/m³.

**Why the examiner asks this:** it is the reason trench collapse kills.

- When a trench wall collapses, the soil comes down with no warning. A worker buried to the chest cannot breathe even if the head is clear.
- Even a small collapse of 1 m³ puts about 2 tonnes on a worker.
- So: excavations about **1.5 m deep or more** need shoring, a trench box, or battered/benched sides (verify the depth against course notes).
- Keep spoil heaps and plant back from the edge. About 1 m minimum is commonly taught (verify).
- Provide safe access (a ladder at least every 7.5 m along the trench is commonly taught, verify), edge barriers, and daily inspection by a competent person before each shift and after rain.

## 2. CDM Regulations 2024

### The basics

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Full title | Occupational Safety and Health (Construction Work) (Design and Management) Regulations 2024 |
| Short name | CDM 2024. Note that the title says "Construction **Work** (Design and Management)" |
| Reference | P.U.(A) 147/2024, made under OSHA 1994 s.66 |
| In force | **1 June 2024**, the same day as the OSHA amendment and the FMA repeal |
| Applies to | Every project that includes construction work, from planning and design to the end of the construction phase |
| Construction work | Building, alteration, repair, maintenance, demolition, site preparation, prefab assembly and installing building services. Mineral extraction is excluded |

### Structure

| Part | Regs | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 1–3 | Preliminary: citation, application, definitions |
| II | 4–9 | Client duties |
| III | 10–17 | Duties of designers, principal designer, contractors, principal contractor |
| IV | 18–37 | General site requirements (demolition, excavation, inspections, welfare, lighting and more) |
| V | 38–39 | Assistants and the **Site Safety Supervisor** (verify heading) |
| Schedules | 1st–4th | 1st: general principles of prevention · 2nd: welfare facilities · 3rd: notification particulars · 4th: particular-risk work |

### The five duty holders

| Role (official term) | Common name | Who it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Client | Any principal or person the project is carried out for |
| Construction work designer | Designer | Anyone who prepares or modifies a design, or instructs someone to |
| **Principal** construction work designer | Principal Designer (PD) | A designer appointed in writing by the client to control the pre-construction phase |
| Construction work contractor | Contractor | Anyone who carries out, manages or controls construction work |
| **Principal** construction work contractor | Principal Contractor (PC) | A contractor appointed in writing by the client to control the construction phase |

Workers (employees and self-employed) must report anything that could endanger themselves or others (Reg 10(5)). Through the PC, they have rights to be consulted and to see relevant information (Reg 16).

### When must the client appoint a PD and PC? (Reg 7)

- The trigger is **more than one contractor**, or when it is reasonably foreseeable that more than one will be on site at any time.
- The appointment must be **in writing**, as soon as practicable **before the construction phase starts**.
- If the client does not appoint them, **the client becomes the PD and/or the PC** and carries those duties.
- With a single contractor, no PD or PC is needed. The sole contractor draws up the construction phase plan.
- **Domestic client** (a homeowner whose project is not part of a business), Reg 9: the client's duties pass to the contractor, or to the PC if there are several contractors. If the domestic client does not appoint, the lead designer becomes the PD and the lead contractor becomes the PC.

### Key duties: who does what

| Duty holder | Main duties | Max fine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client (Regs 4–8) | Give enough **time, money and resources**. Provide welfare facilities. Give **pre-construction information** to designers and contractors. Make sure the CPP exists before work starts and the H&S file is prepared. Check the PD and PC do their jobs. Appoint the PD and PC. **Notify DOSH** | RM500,000 (notification: RM100,000) |
| Designer (Reg 11) | Do not start until the client knows its duties. **Eliminate foreseeable risks through design**. If a risk cannot be eliminated, reduce or control it, **tell the PD**, and get the information into the H&S file | RM200,000 |
| PD (Regs 12, 14) | Plan, manage, monitor and coordinate the **pre-construction phase**. Make sure designers comply. Pass information to the PC. **Prepare and keep the Safety & Health File**, then hand it to the client at completion | RM200,000 (H&S file: RM500,000) |
| PC (Regs 13, 15, 16) | Draw up the **Construction Phase Plan** before site set-up. Plan, manage, monitor and coordinate the **construction phase**. Provide **site induction**. Prevent unauthorised access. Provide welfare. Consult workers. **Appoint a competent Site Safety Supervisor** | RM500,000 |
| Contractor (Reg 17) | Do not start until the client knows its duties. Plan and supervise its own work. Follow the PD/PC and the CPP. Use trained, skilled workers. Give induction | RM500,000 |
| Everyone (Reg 10) | Have the **skills, knowledge, experience** (and organisational capability) for the role. Do not accept a role you cannot meet. Cooperate and share information promptly | RM100,000 |

Most offences carry **up to 1 year's imprisonment** as well as the fine. The Site Safety Supervisor duty under Reg 39 carries RM50,000 or 6 months (verify).

### The three key documents

| Document | Prepared by | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pre-construction information** | Client (helped by PD) | As early as possible | Tells designers and contractors about site hazards, existing services and the condition of existing structures |
| **Construction Phase Plan (CPP)** | PC (or the sole contractor) | Before site set-up, updated throughout | Safety arrangements, site rules and measures for 4th Schedule high-risk work |
| **Safety & Health File** | PD (or PC if there is no PD) | Built during the project, handed to the client at completion | Information needed to maintain, alter or demolish the structure safely later. If the client sells the building, the file goes to the buyer |

### Notifying DOSH (Reg 8)

The client must notify the DG in writing before the construction phase if the work will last **more than 30 working days** or **more than 500 person-days**. The Third Schedule particulars must be **displayed on site** and kept updated. Fine: RM100,000.

Memory aid: **30 / 500**.

### Likely exam questions

1. State the full title and the in-force date of CDM 2024. *(OSH (Construction Work) (Design and Management) Regulations 2024; 1 June 2024)*
2. When must a client appoint a principal designer and principal contractor? What happens if it does not? *(More than one contractor; the client takes on the duties)*
3. List three duties of the client. *(Resources, pre-construction information, CPP and H&S file, appoint PD/PC, notify DOSH)*
4. When must a project be notified to DOSH? *(More than 30 working days or more than 500 person-days)*
5. Who prepares the Construction Phase Plan, and who prepares the Safety & Health File? *(PC; PD)*
6. Explain how a designer reduces risk under CDM 2024. *(Eliminate risk in the design, otherwise reduce or control it, inform the PD, and record it in the H&S file)*
7. What is a domestic client, and who takes on its duties? *(A homeowner not acting in business; the contractor or PC)*
8. Under CDM 2024, who appoints the Site Safety Supervisor? *(The PC, verify Reg 38/39)*

Site link (bridge job): the client (JKR) appoints the PD (the lead design consultant) and the PC (the main contractor). Piling and earthworks subcontractors are "contractors" under the PC's CPP. Embankment and pile-driving risks that cannot be designed out go to the PD and the H&S file.
